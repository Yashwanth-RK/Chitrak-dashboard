# CHITRAK Internal Management Portal

Internal management portal for CHITRAK RVCE.

## Purpose

The portal is intended to centralize CHITRAK's internal operations, including:

- Members
- Teams
- Inventory
- Inventory assignments and history
- Projects
- Sponsors
- Documents
- Activity/audit records
